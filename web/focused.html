Portfolio equity · all three bots

Each line is its own $10,000 portfolio split into equal BTC, ETH and SOL sleeves. Idle cash earns no interest. Daily chart; reported drawdown uses hourly close marks.

Comparable results

Returns are for the whole account, after modeled fees and slippage. A 50% entry cap is not 50% average exposure. Hourly close exposure understates short intrahour holdings.

Controls on the same dates and assets

The controls have different exposure and risk profiles. They measure context, not a risk-matched proof of superiority.

Where the return came from

These crypto assets are correlated and were selected from today's survivors. Three assets are not three independent proofs.

How uncertain is the result?

2,000 resamples of seven-day portfolio return blocks. The interval describes sensitivity within this history; it excludes model-selection uncertainty and is not a forecast.

Costs and delay · reserved test only

Each scenario changes one assumption. Fees and slippage also enter position sizing, so costs can change quantity. Entry risk and all signal rules stay fixed.

Did the proposed improvements help?

Removal tests for the frozen Trend Guard champion on the reserved period. These diagnose the design; they were not used to revise it.

Research gates chosen before the test

Passing would justify further paper research, not live-capital deployment. Trend Guard and Breakout Guard have uncertainty intervals spanning zero. Pullback Guard has only 12 trades.

Execution rules and data limitations

Per asset sleeve: risk budget 0.5% of sleeve equity including modeled round-trip costs; maximum 50% entry exposure; initial/trailing stop 3 ATR14. No leverage, shorts or funding. Protective stops execute using hourly lows; gaps use the worse opening price. Stops only tighten from completed four-hour information. Four-hour cooldown and a reset entry condition after exit.

New entries pause after a 2% daily sleeve loss. A 15% sleeve peak-to-close drawdown triggers exit at the next open and halts the sleeve. These rules do not guarantee a maximum loss during a gap. Base costs are 0.15% fee and 0.05% adverse slippage per side, assumptions rather than a claim about your fee tier.

Current public quantity steps and minimum notional are applied as historical proxies. Stops, liquidity, spread variation, order queue, outages, delistings and taxes cannot be fully reconstructed from hourly candles. The historical holdout is new to this local experiment; it is not prospective or globally unknown market history.
