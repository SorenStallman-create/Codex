Read the exact simulation assumptions and corrections
$10,000 initial cash; BTCUSDT hourly spot; 10% account allocation at entry; 0.15% fee plus 0.05% adverse slippage per side. No leverage or short selling. The same signals and dates are used in each sensitivity run. Buy-and-hold uses the same allocation and costs.
Each week starts in cash, enters according to the prior completed candle target, and forcibly closes remaining holdings at the final close. A first-hour entry can inherit a signal from before the week; the final exit is a test boundary, not the algorithm's exit. Open-position price changes are marked hourly; intrahour drawdown, funding, stops, partial fills and outages are not modeled.
The prior “walk-forward” screen is four fixed 45-day scoring blocks, with no rolling refit. All blocks precede both displayed weeks. Selection among 67 candidates still creates selection risk. The source dataset is preserved and checksum-verified; the weeks have not been updated to a different cutoff.
