Which daily approach held up?
Cash is a 0% / $0 daily control. These results do not prove SPY outperforms crypto: the samples, session lengths and cost assumptions differ.


Higher costs and execution delay
Base, doubled costs and an extra minute of discretionary-order delay. The adverse case is 5× base costs for SPY; for crypto it uses the older 0.15% fee + 0.05% slippage per side. Cost filters and risk sizing can also change which trades qualify.


Rules, data quality and why the models are experimental
Momentum Pullback uses EMA9/21/50, session VWAP and a recent pullback recovery. Volume Breakout requires a prior-channel break plus elevated volume. VWAP Reversion buys a stretched price recovery with a limit on the EMA's decline. Three settings per family were screened before freezing one per family; a separate validation period chose the main candidate.
Initial stop 1.5 ATR14; trend/breakout target 3R; reversion target is last known VWAP. Trades need enough target distance to cover 1.5× estimated round-trip costs. Risk target 0.25% per asset sleeve, 95% cash exposure cap, four entries per asset/day, 15-minute cooldown, 120-minute maximum hold and 1% daily sleeve-loss shutdown. Gaps can exceed intended risk. Long/cash only.
SPY: 09:30–16:00 New York, whole shares, 0.005% fee allowance + 0.01% slippage each side. Crypto: full New York calendar days, three equal sleeves, 0.10% fee + 0.02% slippage each side. These are scenarios, not verified account charges. No leverage, short sales, maker rebates or assumed perfect fills.
SPY: 18 complete public Yahoo minute-data sessions, one warmup, eight development, four validation, five reserved tests. September 3 was excluded before scoring because one minute was missing. Crypto: 393,120 source minute candles, one warmup day plus 90 complete days split 45/15/30. Some market history overlaps prior hourly studies; the newly reserved split applies to these intraday rules.
Stops win any same-minute ambiguity with profit targets. OHLCV cannot model order queues, exact spreads, partial fills or true tick-level drawdown. VWAP is estimated from minute typical prices and volume. The resampled mean-return interval is particularly weak with only five SPY days and excludes model-selection uncertainty.
