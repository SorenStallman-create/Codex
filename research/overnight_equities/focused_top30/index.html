How the cut was made

The broad study produced five model families. I ranked them by validation quality, selected ceil(5 × 30%) = 2 models, and froze that choice before looking at the two replay windows. The selected models are overnight momentum and intraday reversal into the close. The historical run uses 5 bps adverse slippage per side and zero commission. A doubled-cost row uses 10 bps per side.
