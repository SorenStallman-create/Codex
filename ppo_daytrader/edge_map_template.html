BTC edge map

Round 6 of Mosaic Flash. A LightGBM forecaster trained walk-forward on 68 inputs (price, volume, Mosaic indicators, funding, open interest, sentiment), scored on 2021 to Oct 2026 data it never trained on. Question: where, if anywhere, are its predictions strong enough to pay trading costs?

The pre-registered test failed

Rules locked and hash-logged before any code ran. The model picked its horizon and confidence cut-off on validation data each year, then traded the test year once.

The selection was unstable: validation kept choosing the 72-hour horizon with half of all signals, which is mostly "be long while BTC rises". It paid in 2021 and lost in 2022 and 2026.

Where the predictions are good enough

Average move captured per trade, before costs, when the model only acts on its most confident x% of predictions. Pooled over 2021 to 2026, never trained on. A line must sit above the dashed cost line to make money.

The 1-hour horizon never clears even perp costs. 4 hours clears perp costs from the top 20% up. 24 hours clears spot costs at the top 5% and above. Part of the 24h and 72h height is just BTC's upward drift, which the next chart separates out.

4 hours, top 10%: ahead of drift in 5 of 6 years by roughly 10 to 20 bp, but 2026 is negative. 24 hours, top 5%: large in 2022 to 2025 but about 90% of those trades are longs, signals come in clusters on the same days, and 2021 lost.

Volatility matters, the clock mostly doesn't

Gross bp (basis points, 1 bp = 0.01%) per trade on each horizon's top 10% most confident signals, split by how volatile the market was at the time.

Calm thirdMiddle thirdVolatile third

Hour of day: the 24h top-10% edge ranges from 13 to 63 bp across the 24 UTC hours with no stable pattern, which is what noise looks like at roughly 200 trades per hour.

So, the optimal points for prediction

  • Horizon: 4 to 24 hours. Fifteen-minute and one-hour forecasts are real (rank correlation about 0.05) but too small to beat fees.
  • Selectivity: act on only the top 5 to 10% most confident predictions, roughly one or two trades a day, not dozens.
  • Conditions: volatile markets. For 4 to 24 hour holds, the calm two thirds of the time carries roughly half the edge of the volatile third.
  • Not yet an edge: nothing here passed the locked test. The next step is to freeze one rule (for example, a 24h hold on the top 5% of signals, spot, long only) and run it forward on paper.
